# Privacy Policy — DK_Code to HTML

**Effective date:** 2026-10-23
**Developer:** Koteswara Rao
**Contact:** koteswararaomukkapati1996@gmail.com

DK_Code to HTML ("the app") lets you write HTML, CSS and JavaScript on your phone, preview it, and save it as an `.html` file. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Summary

**We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information.** The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## Information the app handles

| Information | What happens to it |
|---|---|
| The code you type or paste (HTML, CSS, JS) and the file name | Stored **only on your device** so your work is still there next time. It is never sent to us. |
| Files you save | Created on your device and handed to the Android share/save sheet **only when you tap Save .html**. Where the file goes is your choice. |
| Clipboard | Read **only when you tap Paste**, and written **only when you tap Copy**. The app never reads the clipboard in the background. |

## Network access

The app itself does not send your data anywhere. The preview shows your own code in a web view. If your code loads something from the internet (for example an image, font or script from a web address), the preview will request it from that site, and that site may see your IP address. That is controlled by the code you write, not by the app.

## Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not share data with third parties.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your choices and deletion

All data is on your device. You can delete it with the **Clear** button in the app, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it in Android settings.

## Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
